Questions

What people usually want to know first.

Does MoneyTempo need my bank login?

No. MoneyTempo does not require your bank username, password or a MoneyTempo account for the core local finance experience.

Why does MoneyTempo request SMS permission?

Automatic financial tracking is a core feature. With your permission, MoneyTempo reads existing and incoming supported financial SMS to identify bank, card and UPI/payment transactions. Core parsing happens on your device.

Does MoneyTempo read every SMS as a transaction?

No. The financial parser is designed to ignore unrelated messages and reject common non-transaction content such as OTPs and promotional messages. Relevant financial messages can be retained locally with the corresponding record so you can inspect the source.

Does MoneyTempo pay bills or move money?

No. Recording a payment in MoneyTempo updates your bookkeeping only. MoneyTempo does not send money to a bank, pay a bill or act as a wallet.

Does MoneyTempoAI send my questions to a cloud AI?

After the optional model is installed on a supported device, supported MoneyTempoAI inference runs on-device. Optional answer reporting is separate: you review the exact text before choosing to submit a report.

Does the investment section connect to my broker?

No. Holdings and current price/NAV values are entered manually in the current release. MoneyTempo does not connect to a broker or execute trades.
