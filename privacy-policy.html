MoneyTempo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026
Privacy and support contact: financemoneytempo@gmail.com

About MoneyTempo

MoneyTempo helps you record and understand your finances. Its transaction database and MoneyTempoAI analysis run on your device. MoneyTempo does not automatically upload your financial records to the developer. Sending an answer report is a separate, explicit choice. Some optional features connect directly to outside services, as described below.

Financial messages and local records

With your permission, MoneyTempo reads existing and incoming SMS to identify financial messages from banks, card providers and payment services. Detection can run while the app is closed. It stores relevant original messages and extracted transactions locally so you can review their source. Unrelated messages are ignored. Disabling SMS permission stops access; manual entry remains available. MoneyTempo does not send messages or delete messages from your phone's SMS app.
Records can include amounts, merchants, account suffixes, balances, categories, dates, notes, portfolio holdings, budgets, goals, tasks, family entries and your optional local profile name/photo. The developer does not receive these records through normal app use. An optional profile name may be stored locally. Upgrading from an earlier test build can retain its locally saved settings; this release does not authenticate a Google account.

Optional location and maps

If you enable transaction location and grant Android permission, the app can attach an approximate or precise current location when you add a transaction or open a recent transaction notification. It does not continuously track you or request background-location access. A stale or unavailable location is skipped.
Online maps are a separate choice, initially off. When enabled, OpenStreetMap receives tile requests identifying the viewed area and the network IP address. Place-name lookup may send coordinates to your device's geocoding provider. Map markers, amounts and SMS are drawn locally, not included in tile requests. Location services themselves may process data under their provider's policies. You can disable transaction location and online maps separately in Profile > Privacy & data; saved locations remain with their records, including recovery copies after a ledger deletion, until local app data is cleared.

Manual investment prices

You enter investment names, identifiers, units, purchase costs and current prices or NAVs yourself. The production portfolio does not search investment websites or fetch market prices. Prices previously saved by an earlier version remain local and are identified by their saved date. A new manual price is saved for today and used for holdings sharing its identifier; entering a price does not change purchase cost or units. Portfolio values use the last saved price, or purchase cost when none exists. These are recordkeeping estimates and may be outdated. Investment records can be included in the local exports described below.

Recent feature searches

When you select a feature from a typed global search, the app keeps up to five recent queries and their feature destinations in its encrypted local database. Use Clear recent searches inside global search to delete this history. History is not sent to analytics, included in exports or backups, or restored from a backup. Clearing local app data or restoring a backup removes it.

Local backup and future features

No MoneyTempo account is required. This release does not offer Google sign-in, access Google Drive, or use Google Block Store for backup keys. Cloud backup may be introduced in a future version. Local JSON/CSV export and JSON import/restore remain available without Google authentication.

Exports and sharing

Sharing a report or export sends the selected content to the app or destination you choose. Explicit plaintext JSON/CSV exports contain readable financial information; keep them secure. The default general JSON export omits raw SMS, transaction coordinates and deleted-entry recovery copies. The current release does not offer the cloud-key-dependent encrypted file export. Keep readable exports secure and use the JSON file import to restore them. App-lock PIN credentials and recent feature-search history are excluded from general exports. Files copied outside the app remain under your control and are not deleted by a local reset.

Storage, security and retention

Financial records are stored in the app's private database using SQLCipher encryption. Encryption failure blocks access instead of deliberately falling back to plaintext on a device. PIN/biometric app lock and redacted lock-screen notifications are supported. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer of app-owned data are excluded; no separate automatic cloud backup runs in this release.
Deleting a ledger transaction removes it from balances and reports but retains an encrypted recovery copy until restored or local app data is cleared. Recovery copies include the original metadata, message and any saved location, and are excluded from the default general JSON export. Use Undo or Settings > Restore last deletion to recover them. Full factory reset removes local recovery copies; it does not delete files exported elsewhere. Profile > Privacy & data > Delete all local data asks Android to clear the app's database, preferences, profile files and caches and close the app. This does not delete original SMS in your messaging app or files exported elsewhere. You can also clear storage or uninstall through Android settings.

Advertising, analytics and advice

This source version includes no developer advertising, remote analytics or crash-upload SDK. Google platform services and external content providers may process operational information under their own policies. MoneyTempoAI provides on-device financial analysis and conversational answers using an optionally downloaded offline model. It is not a cloud chatbot. Its estimates and educational suggestions can be wrong and do not place trades, transfer funds or make payments.

Downloading the optional offline AI model makes HTTPS requests to Hugging Face and its delivery providers. They receive the download request and network information such as your IP address under their own policies. Financial records and chat prompts are not part of the model download request. After installation, model inference runs on your device.

AI chat history is stored in encrypted private storage on your device, with up to 30 days of history and a limit of 200 messages or 2 MB of serialized content, whichever is reached first. Expired messages are removed when history is accessed and during scheduled background cleanup; Android may delay background cleanup while the app is not active. You can delete chat history from the chat screen without deleting financial records or the downloaded model. Saved answers are dated snapshots and may differ from your current records. Chat history is excluded from Android backup and MoneyTempo financial-data exports.

Answer reports

When in-app reporting is configured, you can choose a reason, edit the answer excerpt, add optional details and consent to send that text to MoneyTempo for review. Reports include an anonymous random report reference and app version. Your question, full chat history, ledger, email and device identifier are not attached automatically. The excerpt itself may contain financial or other private information; remove it before sending if you do not want it included. The app confirms submission only after Google accepts the email send request and the service returns a matching reference; this does not guarantee inbox delivery or review. A build without a configured service cannot submit reports.
The publisher-controlled reporting service uses Google Apps Script to email the report to financemoneytempo@gmail.com. Google processes submitted content and request/network information under its policies (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Report content is used for quality and safety review. Support manually permanently deletes report emails, including any sent copies, within 30 days; the send-only script does not automatically delete mailbox messages. The script stores only references, content hashes, timestamps and submission states for retry detection for seven days, removed on the next request after expiry; it does not store answer text in Script Properties. It also stores a rolling daily submission budget, without network-address or device identifiers. Provider operational/recovery copies may have different retention. Reporting is optional and does not change your ledger or hide an answer. Contact financemoneytempo@gmail.com with your report reference to request removal; deleting local app data alone does not delete an already submitted report.

Support and changes

If you contact financemoneytempo@gmail.com, your email provider and ours process the message you choose to send. Avoid sending raw bank messages, account numbers or backups unless necessary for a specific support request. We use support correspondence to respond to you. This policy should be updated when the app's data handling changes; the current public version must also be linked from the Play Store listing.
