MoneyTempo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026
Privacy and support contact: financemoneytempo@gmail.com

About MoneyTempo

MoneyTempo helps you record and understand your finances. Its transaction database and MoneyTempoAI analysis run on your device. MoneyTempo does not operate a developer server that receives your financial records. Some optional features connect directly to outside services, as described below.

Financial messages and local records

With your permission, MoneyTempo reads existing and incoming SMS to identify financial messages from banks, card providers and payment services. Detection can run while the app is closed. It stores relevant original messages and extracted transactions locally so you can review their source. Unrelated messages are ignored. Disabling SMS permission stops access; manual entry remains available. MoneyTempo does not send messages or delete messages from your phone's SMS app.
Records can include amounts, merchants, account suffixes, balances, categories, dates, notes, portfolio holdings, budgets, goals, tasks, family entries and your optional local profile name/photo. The developer does not receive these records through normal app use. A name or Google email may be stored locally; it would be inaccurate to say the app never stores names or email addresses.

Optional location and maps

If you enable transaction location and grant Android permission, the app can attach an approximate or precise current location when you add a transaction or open a recent transaction notification. It does not continuously track you or request background-location access. A stale or unavailable location is skipped.
Online maps are a separate choice, initially off. When enabled, OpenStreetMap or Esri receives tile requests identifying the viewed area and the network IP address. Place-name lookup may send coordinates to your device's geocoding provider. Map markers, amounts and SMS are drawn locally, not included in tile requests. Location services themselves may process data under their provider's policies. You can disable transaction location and online maps separately in Profile > Privacy & data; saved locations remain with their records, including recovery copies after a ledger deletion, until local app data is cleared.

Manual investment prices

You enter investment names, identifiers, units, purchase costs and current prices or NAVs yourself. The production portfolio does not search investment websites or fetch market prices. Prices previously saved by an earlier version remain local and are identified by their saved date. A new manual price is saved for today and used for holdings sharing its identifier; entering a price does not change purchase cost or units. Portfolio values use the last saved price, or purchase cost when none exists. These are recordkeeping estimates and may be outdated. Investment records can be included in the optional personal backup or exports described below.

Recent feature searches

When you select a feature from a typed global search, the app keeps up to five recent queries and their feature destinations in its encrypted local database. Use Clear recent searches inside global search to delete this history. History is not sent to analytics, included in exports or backups, or restored from a backup. Clearing local app data or restoring a backup removes it.

Exports and sharing

Sharing a report or export sends the selected content to the app or destination you choose. Encrypted backup export is available. Explicit plaintext JSON/CSV exports contain readable financial information; keep them secure. The default general JSON export omits raw SMS, transaction coordinates and deleted-entry recovery copies. Full encrypted backups retain these records for recovery. App-lock PIN credentials and recent feature-search history are excluded from both. Files copied outside the app remain under your control and are not deleted by a local reset.

Storage, security and retention

Financial records are stored in the app's private database using SQLCipher encryption. Encryption failure blocks access instead of deliberately falling back to plaintext on a device. PIN/biometric app lock and redacted lock-screen notifications are supported. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer of app-owned data are excluded; no separate automatic cloud backup runs in this release.

Deleting a ledger transaction removes it from balances and reports but retains an encrypted recovery copy until restored or local app data is cleared. Recovery copies include the original metadata, message and any saved location, and are excluded from the default general JSON export. Use Undo or Data Management > Restore last deletion to recover them.

Full factory reset in Data Management removes local recovery copies; it does not delete files exported elsewhere. Privacy & data links to Data Management for local-data export, recovery and reset controls. Full factory reset asks Android to clear the app's database, preferences, profile files and caches and close the app.

This does not delete original SMS in your messaging app or files exported elsewhere. You can also clear MoneyTempo's storage or uninstall the app through Android settings.

Advertising, analytics and advice

This source version includes no developer advertising, remote analytics or crash-upload SDK. Google platform services and external content providers may process operational information under their own policies. MoneyTempoAI is on-device rule-based financial analysis, not a cloud chatbot. Its estimates and educational suggestions can be wrong and do not place trades, transfer funds or make payments.

Support and changes

If you contact financemoneytempo@gmail.com, your email provider and ours process the message you choose to send. Avoid sending raw bank messages, account numbers or backups unless necessary for a specific support request. We use support correspondence to respond to you. This policy should be updated when the app's data handling changes; the current public version must also be linked from the Play Store listing.
