MoneyTempo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026
Privacy and support contact: financemoneytempo@gmail.com

Privacy in brief: MoneyTempo is designed to keep your core financial records on your device. We do not require a MoneyTempo account, and we do not automatically upload your financial SMS, transaction history, budgets, portfolio records, or MoneyTempoAI chat history to our servers. Some optional features connect to external services only when you choose to use them, as explained below.

1. Financial SMS and local financial records

With your permission, MoneyTempo can read existing and incoming SMS messages to identify financial messages from banks, card providers, UPI services, wallets, and other payment services. This can include SMS processing while the app is closed.

Relevant financial messages and the transactions extracted from them are stored locally on your device so you can review their source. Unrelated messages are ignored. If you disable SMS permission, MoneyTempo stops accessing SMS and you can continue using manual transaction entry.

MoneyTempo does not send SMS messages on your behalf and does not delete messages from your phone's messaging app.

Local financial records may include amounts, merchants, account or card suffixes, balances, categories, dates, notes, budgets, goals, portfolio records, manual investment prices, tasks, family entries, and related financial calculations.

2. Profile name and profile photo

You can optionally add a profile name and choose a profile photo. These are stored locally on your device. MoneyTempo uses Android's system photo picker, so you choose the specific image you want to use rather than granting broad access to your photo library.

The selected profile image is copied into MoneyTempo's private app storage and is not automatically uploaded.

3. Location and maps

Location is optional and off by default. If you enable transaction location and grant Android permission, MoneyTempo can attach an approximate or precise current location when you add a transaction or open a recent transaction notification. MoneyTempo does not request background-location access and does not continuously track your location.

Online maps are a separate optional feature. When enabled, OpenStreetMap may receive requests that identify the map area you are viewing together with normal network information such as your IP address. Place-name lookup may send coordinates to the geocoding provider available on your device.

Transaction amounts, financial SMS content, and map markers are not included in map tile requests. You can disable transaction location and online maps separately in MoneyTempo's privacy settings.

4. Manual investment records

MoneyTempo's portfolio features are for personal recordkeeping. You enter investment names, identifiers, quantities, purchase costs, and current prices or NAVs yourself.

The current release does not connect to a broker, place trades, custody assets, or automatically fetch live investment prices. Portfolio records and calculations stay local unless you explicitly export them.

5. Search history

MoneyTempo can keep a small recent history of feature searches on your device to make navigation faster. This history is stored locally, is not sent to an analytics service, and can be cleared from within the app.

6. Import, export, and sharing

MoneyTempo supports local CSV and JSON import/export. File selection and save locations are chosen by you through Android's system file interfaces.

Plaintext CSV or JSON exports can contain readable financial information, so keep exported files secure. Files saved or shared outside MoneyTempo remain under your control and are not removed when you clear MoneyTempo's local app data.

MoneyTempo does not provide Google Drive backup or Google sign-in in the current release. Cloud backup may be introduced in a future version, in which case this policy will be updated before that feature is made available.

7. MoneyTempoAI

MoneyTempoAI is designed to run on your device. The optional AI model is downloaded only if you choose to install it. The model download is made over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its delivery providers.

Download providers receive the normal information required to deliver the file, such as network request information and IP address. Your financial records and MoneyTempoAI prompts are not included in the model download request.

After the model is installed, MoneyTempoAI analysis and chat inference run on your device. We do not send normal MoneyTempoAI prompts or your local chat history to a cloud AI service.

AI chat history is stored in MoneyTempo's private encrypted storage for up to 30 days, subject to a maximum of 200 messages or 2 MB of serialized history, whichever limit is reached first. You can delete chat history from the chat screen.

8. Optional MoneyTempoAI answer reports

If you choose to report a MoneyTempoAI answer, MoneyTempo lets you review and edit the answer excerpt, choose a reason, add optional details, and confirm before anything is sent.

A submitted report can include the text you choose to send, an anonymous random report reference, and the app version. Your full question, full chat history, ledger, email address, and device identifier are not automatically attached.

Because an answer excerpt can contain financial or other private information, review it carefully and remove anything you do not want to submit.

MoneyTempo sends reports over HTTPS to our Google Apps Script reporting service, which forwards the report to financemoneytempo@gmail.com. Google processes the submitted content and normal request/network information under Google's own privacy terms.

We use submitted reports for quality and safety review. We permanently delete report email content, including sent copies, within 30 days. Short-lived report references, hashes, timestamps, and submission-state records used for retry protection are retained for up to seven days after expiry and do not contain the submitted answer text.

Reporting is optional and does not change your financial records or normal MoneyTempoAI use.

9. Advertising, analytics, and crash reporting

The current release does not include advertising, developer analytics, or a crash-upload SDK. Google Play and external service providers may process operational information under their own policies when you use their platform or an optional connected feature.

10. Security and local storage

Financial records are stored in MoneyTempo's private database using SQLCipher encryption. MoneyTempo does not intentionally fall back to plaintext storage if database encryption fails.

MoneyTempo also supports an optional PIN or biometric app lock and supports redacted lock-screen notifications.

Android automatic backup and device transfer of MoneyTempo-owned app data are disabled for this release.

11. Deleting your data

You can delete MoneyTempo's local app data from:

  • MoneyTempo > Privacy & data > Data Management > Full Factory Reset & Clean State, or
  • Android Settings > Apps > MoneyTempo > Storage > Clear storage.

Clearing MoneyTempo's local data removes the app's database, preferences, profile files, cached files, and other MoneyTempo-owned local data. It does not delete the original SMS messages from your phone's messaging app and does not delete files you previously exported outside MoneyTempo.

Deleting an individual ledger transaction removes it from balances and reports but keeps an encrypted recovery copy until you restore it or clear MoneyTempo's local app data.

If you previously submitted a MoneyTempoAI answer report and want it removed, email financemoneytempo@gmail.com and include the anonymous report reference shown when the report was submitted.

You can also review the dedicated MoneyTempo Data Deletion page.

12. Support correspondence

If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours process the message you choose to send. Please avoid sending raw bank SMS messages, full account numbers, backup files, or other sensitive financial information unless it is necessary for a specific support request.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when MoneyTempo's features or data-handling practices change. The current version will remain available from the Google Play listing and the MoneyTempo website.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests, contact: financemoneytempo@gmail.com
