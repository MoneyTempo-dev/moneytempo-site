MoneyTempo Data Deletion

MoneyTempo does not require a MoneyTempo account. Most financial information, SMS-derived transactions, budgets, settings, locations and MoneyTempoAI history are stored locally on your device.

Delete data stored on your device

To permanently delete MoneyTempo's local app data:

  1. Open MoneyTempo.
  2. Open Privacy & data.
  3. Open Data Management.
  4. Select Full Factory Reset & Clean State.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

This clears MoneyTempo's app data, including its local financial database, locally stored financial SMS copies, transaction locations, profile information, settings, cached files and other locally stored MoneyTempo data.

You can also delete the same local app data through Android: Settings > Apps > MoneyTempo > Storage > Clear storage.

Data that is not deleted by a MoneyTempo reset

Deleting MoneyTempo's local data does not delete the original SMS messages from your phone's messaging app. It also does not delete files that you previously exported or shared outside MoneyTempo.

MoneyTempo does not provide Google Drive backup or Google sign-in in the current release.

Delete a submitted MoneyTempoAI report

If you voluntarily submitted a MoneyTempoAI answer report, contact financemoneytempo@gmail.com and include the anonymous report reference shown when the report was submitted.

Submitted report email content is permanently deleted within 30 days. Short-lived report references, hashes, timestamps and submission-state records used for retry protection are retained for up to seven days after expiry and do not contain the submitted answer text.
